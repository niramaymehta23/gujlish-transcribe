# Channel profile

- **Channel:** Harisena. Posts pravachans and goshthis of Guruhari Prabodh Swamiji, with English subtitles.
- **Audience:** teenagers who are mildly spiritual, and seekers (mumukshus) not deeply involved with satsang. They are comfortable in English and may not know the terms or the people.
- **Speakers:** Guruhari Prabodh Swamiji, the present (pragat) guru. His guru is Guruhari Hariprasad Swamiji, who went to Akshardham on 26 July 2021, so speak of him in the past tense. Before diksha, Guruhari Hariprasad Swamiji was Prabhudasbhai, and anecdotes of Prabhudasbhai tell his youth. Other saints include Brahmavihari Swami.
- **Title style:** clear and curiosity-driven, true to the talk, sentence case, no emojis, no clickbait. Say what the viewer gets.
- **Description style:** warm and respectful. Explain any term a newcomer would not know.

## Names

- **Guruhari Prabodh Swamiji** is the official form. Use it in full the first time in any title, description or video. "Prabodh Swamiji" is two words, never "Prabodhswamiji" or "Parbodhswamiji". The talks also say "Guruhari Prabodh Swamiji Maharaj", "Prabodh Swamiji" or just "Guruhari": all of these mean him. Before diksha he was Narottambhai.
- **Guruhari Hariprasad Swamiji** is the official form for his guru. Before diksha he was Prabhudasbhai.
- **"Swamiji" and "Guruhari" on their own** are kept exactly as said. Never expand them into a full name; the audience knows who is meant. As a guide to the meaning: "Swamiji" usually means Guruhari Hariprasad Swamiji, and "Guruhari" usually means Guruhari Prabodh Swamiji. When Guruhari Prabodh Swamiji himself is speaking, "Guruhari" means his guru, Guruhari Hariprasad Swamiji, because he almost never refers to himself.
- **Other names, spelled this way:** Bhagwan Swaminarayan (also called Shreeji Maharaj), Gunatitanand Swami, Bhagatji Maharaj, Jaga Swami, Shastriji Maharaj, Yogiji Maharaj, Ramanand Swami, Gopalanand Swami, Brahmavihari Swami, Prabhudasbhai, Akshardham, Atmiya Vidya Mandir.
- **Terms, spelled this way:** Harisena, satsang, goshthi, pravachan, agna, mumukshu, Pradeshik Sant, upasana, diksha, parampara.

## Upasana

Akshar Purushottam upasana: Bhagwan Swaminarayan is Purna Purushottam Narayan, and Gunatitanand Swami is Mul Akshar. The guru parampara, in order: Bhagwan Swaminarayan, Gunatitanand Swami, Bhagatji Maharaj, Shastriji Maharaj, Yogiji Maharaj, Guruhari Hariprasad Swamiji, Guruhari Prabodh Swamiji.

Never compare, rank or describe them differently from this. Never say anything about them that the talk itself does not say.

Sutras the talks may quote:
- Guruhari Hariprasad Swamiji: "Yuvako mari pooja che, yuvako maru sarvasva" (the youth are my worship, the youth are my everything).
- Guruhari Prabodh Swamiji: seva, smruti, suhradbhav and swadharm, and "Hu toh nimit matra" (I am only an instrument).
